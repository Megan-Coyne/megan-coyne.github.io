My Interests

As I begin my career, I'm exploring a wide range of interests. Below are a few topics I find particularly fascinating, some I hope to explore further through formal research, possibly during a PhD, while others interest me for their corporate applications. I'd be happy to connect and discuss any of these areas, as I am excited to learn and explore!

Academic Research Interests

Computational Geometry
I am deeply interested in designing efficient algorithms for spatial problems, from geometric optimization to spatial data analysis. This interest stems from a higher geometry course where we explored fascinating shapes, proved their properties, and studied their structures. I often thought that writing code (highly complicated code that is) to model and analyze these figures could provide an even bigger picture, bridging theory and computation. Having studied a significant amount of topology through my complex and real analysis courses, I hope to apply it to computer science through computational topology. I enjoy this balance between rigorous mathematical reasoning and practical applications, much like the work I am doing for my thesis.

High Performance Computing
I find it compelling how high performance computing allows us to make computational breakthroughs that would otherwise be impossible. As a math student, I know the satisfaction of spending hours on a single problem, which has given me a deep appreciation for computational tools that can unlock new avenues for discovery. High performance computing allows us to tackle problems beyond a single machine, from large-scale simulations to analyzing massive datasets for applications like climate prediction. I am excited by its potential to accelerate discovery and innovation and would love to contribute to advancing at least one of these applications.

Quantum Computing
Quantum computing fascinates me for its potential to tackle problems that are intractable with classical methods. I am eager to explore both its theoretical foundations and practical applications, especially in optimization, cryptography, and advanced algorithmic research. The prospect of hybrid quantum-classical systems is exciting, as they could combine the strengths of both approaches to enable entirely new types of computation. I am intrigued that current quantum methods are not yet ready for real-world use. It feels like the beginning of something novel, similar to the early days of complex numbers, and I would love to be among those exploring it.

Career Interests

All Things Security
With a concentration in cyber defense, I enjoy exploring how to design, test, and secure systems against evolving threats, from network security to secure software engineering practices. This past academic year, I took Secure Application Development and Networks and Security courses where we studied different cyber attacks, simulated the role of the attacker by hacking into our peers’ applications and servers, and worked to stop them from gaining access in real time.

Product Development
I’m passionate about building software solutions from the ground up, transforming ideas into working products that solve real problems. I deeply enjoy the process of dedicating months to a project and finally seeing something tangible take shape. There’s nothing quite like that feeling, I’m actually feeling it right now as I craft this very website :)

Quantitative Analysis
Through my undergraduate thesis, I applied advanced statistical concepts like Extreme Value Theory to analyze stock behavior and predict future trends. I found that I enjoy this work far more than I expected and am keen to explore related career opportunities in the future.

Looking Ahead

I am always open to exploring new areas and expanding my skill set. As I grow professionally, I know my focus will continue to evolve, and I am excited to see where curiosity and opportunity will take me!
